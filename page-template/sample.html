Frequently Asked Questions

Where is SHU located if we are visiting from Footscray?

We are at 147 Johnston Street, Collingwood, VIC 3066. If you are searching for vegan Yum Cha near Footscray, you can join us in Collingwood for a modern Sichuan weekend lunch.

When do we serve unlimited vegan Yum Cha?

We serve unlimited vegan Yum Cha at lunch on Saturdays and Sundays, from 12pm to 3pm. Each sitting lasts two hours, and we recommend booking ahead to secure your preferred weekend.

How does the unlimited Yum Cha format work?

Your table enjoys nine signature dishes cooked to order and served continuously for two hours. Expect a rotating mix of dumplings, baos, noodles and Sichuan-style small plates, so everyone can share and try a little of everything.

Can we request dietary changes for Yum Cha?

We do not cater for dietary requests or allergies for Yum Cha. Every dish is fully vegan, but if you need changes for gluten, allium or nuts, our tasting menus may suit you better, so contact us before booking.

Can we add drinks to our Yum Cha booking?

Yes, we offer an optional bottomless beverage package that can be added to your Yum Cha booking. Please note we do not offer BYO.

How do we reserve a table?

We accept bookings online through our reservation page or by phone on 03 9090 7878. We recommend reserving ahead, particularly for weekend Yum Cha.
